## Recommendations

Use this section to summarize recommendations to the IT manager based on the risk posed if the controls or compliance best practices are not implemented in a timely manner.

- **Least Privilege and Role-Based Access:** Implement a strict user hierarchy and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to separate duties and restrict sensitive access to authorized personnel only.
- **Disaster Recovery and Data Backups:** Develop a comprehensive Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery (BCDR) plan, including regular automated off-site backups to ensure rapid data restoration after a breach or system failure.
- **Strong Password and Authentication Policies:** Implement centralized password management, enforce strict password policies, and mandate Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) across all systems.
- **Network Monitoring:** Deploy an Intrusion Detection System (IDS) to monitor network traffic for suspicious activity and potential security threats in real time.
- **Security Staffing and Compliance Training:** Hire specialized IT security personnel or a Security Operations Center (SOC) team, and conduct staff training to implement end-to-end encryption, maintain PCI DSS compliance for payment processing, and align with GDPR regulations for handling European Union customer data.
