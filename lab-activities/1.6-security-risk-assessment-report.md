# Security Risk Assessment Report

## Part 1: Select up to three hardening tools and methods to implement

### Recommended Hardening Tools and Methods
1. Password policies
2. Firewall maintenance
3. Multifactor Authentication (MFA)

---

## Part 2: Explain your recommendations

Based on the recent breach assessment, four major vulnerabilities were identified that require immediate hardening.

* **Password policies** will standardize, guide, and enforce rules for employee accounts, especially admin or elevated accounts, to be secure in their everyday use. They adhere to NIST standards, preventing unauthorized access through brute force or credential sharing.
* **Firewall maintenance** should be conducted daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly depending on the depth of configuration required.
* **Multifactor Authentication (MFA)** since the company already has MFA available, it should be fully deployed to layer protection onto every employee's account to block unauthorized access even if their passwords are leaked.
